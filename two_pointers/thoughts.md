Types of two pointer problems.

<b>Opposite direction</b>:
* Array is sorted and trying to find a pair or combination
* Comparing symmetric structures e.g. palindromes
* Avoid nested loops when looking at all pairs

Typically very useful in sorted arrays because you can eliminate an entire range of possibilities.
Instead of checking all possibilities with a nested loop you can do it with just one loop.

Two pointer is useful because you can reduce the number of iterations needed, track a relationship between two places,
and avoid extra space.

Two pointers typically come up in
* Palindromes
* Reversals
* Merging sorted data
* "K" sized comparisons
